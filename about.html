About the association

Who we are, what we do, and why we exist.

Vision

A strong and sustainable alumni community advancing excellence at Kyuso Boys High School and society at large.

Mission

To contribute towards meaningful development of Kyuso Boys High School, the alumni members and the school community through connecting alumni, promoting mentorship, mobilizing resources and supporting education.

Values

  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Accountability
  • Professionalism
  • Transparency
  • Service
  • Inclusivity
  • Respect

Objectives

  1. To unite former students of Kyuso Boys High School.
  2. To mobilize alumni members towards extending support to alumni members during times of need.
  3. To foster lifelong fellowship and networking among alumni members.
  4. To support academic excellence and quality education.
  5. To support educational, infrastructural and co-curricular support to Kyuso Boys Secondary School (Alma Mater).
  6. To establish and administer scholarships, bursaries and mentorship programmes.
  7. To mobilize financial and non-financial resources towards the objects of the association.
  8. To support needy and deserving students.
  9. To preserve and promote the traditions and heritage of the school.
  10. To support student welfare and talent development.
  11. To preserve the history, traditions and heritage of the school.
  12. To collaborate with the Board of Management, Principal and other stakeholders of Kyuso Boys High School for the benefit of alumni members and the school.
  13. To organize alumni reunions, conferences and networking events.
  14. To undertake charitable and community development projects benefiting Kyuso Boys High School and its environs.
  15. To promote professional growth and leadership development among alumni members.

What we do

We organize reunions, share school news, maintain a directory so classmates can find each other, and support scholarship and development efforts at the school.
